# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes empathy, respectful feedback, accepting
responsibility, and focusing on what is best for the community. Unacceptable
behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention,
trolling, publishing private information, or other conduct inappropriate in a
professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through
[GitHub private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/luutru433-coder/local-knowledge-suite/security/advisories/new)
with the title `Code of Conduct report`. Do not expose a reporter or affected
person in a public issue.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, and other
contributions that violate this Code, and may temporarily or permanently ban
participants.
