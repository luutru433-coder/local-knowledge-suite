# Northstar Bicycle Labs product catalog

Status: fictional training material

## Comet City

Comet City is a fictional aluminum commuter bicycle. It includes integrated
lights, a rear rack, and eight speeds. Available frame sizes are S, M, and L.

## Aurora Trail

Aurora Trail is a fictional hardtail trail bicycle. It includes a suspension
fork, hydraulic disc brakes, and twelve speeds. Available frame sizes are M,
L, and XL.

## Warranty

Frames have a fictional 24-month limited warranty from the delivery date.
Wear items such as tires, brake pads, and chains are excluded. Warranty service
requires an order number and an inspection by an authorized fictional Northstar
technician.
