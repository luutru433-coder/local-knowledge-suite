# Northstar Bicycle Labs support policy

Status: fictional training material

## Standard bicycle returns

An unused standard bicycle may be returned within 30 calendar days of delivery.
The customer must provide the order number. The bicycle must have no road wear
and must include the original accessories.

## Custom-built bicycles

Custom-built bicycles cannot be returned for a change of mind. If a custom
bicycle arrives damaged, the customer must contact support within seven
calendar days of delivery and provide photographs of the packaging and damage.

## Support hours

Support operates Monday through Friday, 09:00–17:00 in the fictional Northstar
local time zone. Requests received outside those hours enter the next business
day's queue.
